# Content Quality Standards — Agri Learning Point

**Brand:** Agri Learning Point • BY SATYAM SIR & TEAM  
**Audience:** UPSSSC AGTA, IBPS AFO, ICAR, NABARD, FCI, and State Agriculture Examinations  
**Edition Format:** Daily Bilingual (English + Hindi)  

---

## 1. Editorial Principles

1. **Authenticity & Verification:** Every published story must originate from an official or credible news source (PIB, Ministry releases, ICAR, RBI, SEBI, DD News, AIR, national dailies).
2. **Competitive Exam Relevance:** News items must carry educational and examination value. Priority is given to:
   - Agricultural policies, schemes, MSP revisions, crop yields, and ICAR initiatives.
   - Central government programmes, financial allocations, and nodal ministries.
   - Statutory and regulatory decisions (RBI, SEBI, CCPA, CCI, CTI, APEDA, NABARD).
   - Scientific, environmental, and technological breakthroughs.
   - Important national and international treaties, summits, and indices.
3. **Strict Fact Grounding:** Never invent figures, dates, scheme names, or officeholders. Missing details must never be fabricated to fill space.
4. **Timezone & Date Integrity:** Edition boundaries and publication dates must follow `Asia/Kolkata` (IST). News must reflect current developments without presenting stale items as today's news.

---

## 2. Bilingual Editorial Standards

### English Standards
- Direct, grammatically sound, active academic voice.
- No dangling prepositions, incomplete headline fragments, or raw datelines (`Updated - November 17, 2026...`).
- Consistent terminology and official designations.

### Hindi Standards
- Clear, natural, standard Hindi suitable for competitive government exams.
- Devanagari numerals or standard Roman figures formatted consistently (₹1.27 लाख करोड़, 37.5 लाख किसान).
- Accurate official Hindi names for ministries, institutions, and schemes (e.g. उपभोक्ता मामले, खाद्य एवं सार्वजनिक वितरण मंत्रालय; भारतीय कृषि अनुसंधान परिषद).
- Zero broken grammar constructs (e.g. avoiding awkward literal translations like "ने में").

### Equivalence Validation
- English and Hindi texts must communicate the exact same factual information.
- Numbers, financial outlays, and entity names must align identically in both languages.

## 3. Section Taxonomy & Distinct Purpose

| Section | Target Item Count | Purpose | Content Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Top Stories (1–15)** | Exactly 15 | In-depth daily current affairs study units | Headline, Source, Photo/Graphic, MCQ, 5 Options, Detailed Bilingual Explanation, Contextual Static GK |
| **Agriculture Corner** | 2–4 | Specialized agricultural exam preparation | High-yield takeaways for AGTA/AFO/ICAR (Agronomy, Horticulture, Schemes, Institutes) |
| **Quick Bites** | 6–10 | Secondary news developments not covered in top stories | 2-line concise bilingual summary categorized by domain (National, Economy, Science, Sports) |
| **One-Liners** | 10–15 | Rapid active-recall revision facts | Distilled factual statements (Founding years, Acts, Outlays, Headquarters, Committees) |
| **Rapid Revision Key** | 15 rows | Quick self-assessment answer key | Question number, correct option, exam topic, category |
